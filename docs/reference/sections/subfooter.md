# Subfooter

> **File:** `sections/subfooter.liquid` · **Area:** Header & footer · **Size:** 1092 lines

## What it does

The thin bottom strip: logo, copyright text, legal links and payment method icons. It can show a ready-made payment icons banner image or individual icons that you can switch on, and add your own custom icons as blocks.

**Typical uses**

- Show Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, iDEAL, Bancontact and other accepted payments.
- Edit the copyright line and extra legal text.

## Where it appears

- Footer group (every page)

## How to edit it

1. Open **Online Store > Themes > Customize**.
2. Scroll to the very top (header group) or bottom (footer group) of any page and click the section.
3. Change the settings on the left; changes preview live. Click **Save**.

## Tips
